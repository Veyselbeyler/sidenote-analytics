Questions

Before you sign in

Can you see my Analytics data?

No. SideNote has no server. Your browser gets the numbers straight from Google, and they never leave it.

Google's sign-in screen says it can "see your Google Analytics data". What does that mean?

That's Google's standard wording for read-only access. SideNote can read your reports, but it can't change or delete anything. You can remove its access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Does it work on any website?

It works on any site you can see in your own GA4. On other sites, the panel simply tells you there's no data for it.

What if my site has several GA4 properties or subdomains?

SideNote picks the right one for you. If it isn't sure, it tells you, and you can choose another from the list.

Why is it free?

There's no server to pay for, so there's nothing to charge for. No plans, no trials, no ads.
