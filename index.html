How it works

Get started in three steps.

Once SideNote Analytics is live on the Chrome Web Store:

  1. 1Add it to Chrome

    Install SideNote Analytics from the Chrome Web Store and pin it to your toolbar.

  2. 2Sign in with Google

    Use the account you use for GA4. SideNote gets read-only access, and you can disconnect it at any time.

  3. 3Open a page on your site

    Click the SideNote icon to see that page's numbers. It finds the matching GA4 property automatically.

Questions

Before you sign in

Can the developer see my Analytics data?

No. Your browser retrieves the numbers directly from Google. SideNote doesn't send them to me or to any server.

Google's sign-in screen says it can "see and download your Google Analytics data". What does that mean?

That's Google's standard wording for read-only access. SideNote can read your reports, but it can't change or delete anything. You can remove its access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Does it work on any website?

It works on sites tracked by a GA4 property your Google account can access. If SideNote can't find a matching property, the panel lets you know.

What if my site has several GA4 properties or subdomains?

SideNote automatically looks for a matching property. If there's more than one possible match, you can choose from the list.

Why is it free?

I built SideNote Analytics as a portfolio project and wanted it to be useful to other people who work with GA4. It's free, with no paid plans, trials or ads.
