How it works

Connect once. Explore as you browse.

Once SideNote is available, getting started takes three steps.

  1. 1Add it to Chrome

    Install SideNote Analytics from the Chrome Web Store and pin it to your toolbar.

  2. 2Connect Google Analytics

    Sign in with the Google account you use for GA4. Access is read-only, and you can revoke it at any time.

  3. 3Open a page on your site

    Click the SideNote icon. It finds the matching GA4 property and shows that page's numbers in the side panel.

Questions

Before you connect

Can the developer see my Analytics data?

No. SideNote runs in your browser and gets data directly from Google. It never sends your Analytics data to me or to any server.

Why does Google ask for access to my Analytics data?

SideNote needs permission to read your reports so it can show your page's numbers. That access is read-only: it can't change or delete anything in your Analytics account. You can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Does it work on any website?

It works on sites tracked by a GA4 property your Google account can access. If SideNote can't find a matching property, the panel lets you know.

What if my site has multiple GA4 properties or subdomains?

SideNote automatically looks for a matching property. If there's more than one possible match, you can choose from the list.

Why is it free?

I built SideNote as a portfolio project and wanted to share it with other people who work with GA4. It's free, with no paid plans, trials or ads.
