Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

SideNote Analytics is a Chrome extension that shows Google Analytics 4 figures for the page you are viewing. This policy explains what it reads, where that goes and what it keeps.

The short version. SideNote Analytics has no server. It does not collect, sell or share your data, and it does not track you. It talks only to Google's Analytics APIs, on your behalf, with read-only access.

What it reads

  • Your Google Analytics data, read-only. When you sign in, Chrome gives the extension a token with the analytics.readonly scope. It uses that token to list the GA4 properties you can access and to request reports for the page you are on. It cannot change anything in your Analytics account.
  • The address of the tab where you opened the panel. The page's host and path are used to find the matching property and to filter the reports to that page. The extension does not read the page's content and injects no code into it.

Where it goes

Requests go directly from your browser to Google:

  • analyticsdata.googleapis.com, the GA4 Data API, for reports
  • analyticsadmin.googleapis.com, the GA4 Admin API, for your property list

Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of its own.

What it keeps, and where

All of it stays in your browser's extension storage.

WhatWhereHow long
Report responseschrome.storage.session5 to 30 minutes (property metadata up to 12 hours); cleared when the browser closes
Your property list and the property you picked for a sitechrome.storage.localA week for the list; your picks until you remove the extension
Your quiz score for each pagechrome.storage.localUntil you remove the extension
Your chosen cards and date rangechrome.storage.syncSynced by Chrome across your own signed-in browsers

Removing the extension deletes all of it. Your Google sign-in token is managed by Chrome, not stored by the extension. You can revoke its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

"Challenge a friend" copies a line of text with your quiz score to your clipboard. It does not include the site or page, and the extension does not send it anywhere; you decide whether to paste it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SideNote Analytics' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
